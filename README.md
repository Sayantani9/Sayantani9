**Artificial Intelligence • Machine Learning • Computer Vision • Robotics • IoT • Sustainability • Research • Software Engineering • Entrepreneurship**

I like taking ideas through the complete journey:


# 🚀 CURRENTLY BUILDING — YZ!

## WHY YZ? / YZ!

### **A Gen-Z technology & innovation ecosystem under Sanghamitra Ventures**

I am currently building **YZ!**, a platform focused on connecting **fresh minds, student innovators, AI builders and companies** to turn real-world problems into meaningful technology.

The idea is simple:

> **Why not Gen-Z?**

YZ! explores how young builders can contribute directly to company projects, product ideation, research, AI solutions and emerging technology.

Instead of waiting for ideas to become opportunities, we are trying to build the bridge between:

```text
STUDENT
   +
FRESH IDEA
   +
RESEARCH
   +
AI / TECHNOLOGY
   +
REAL BUSINESS PROBLEM
        ↓
      YZ!
        ↓
PROJECT
        ↓
PROTOTYPE
        ↓
IMPACT
```

### What I am working around

* 🤖 AI & intelligent systems
* 🧠 AI agents and automation
* 👁️ Computer Vision
* 🌱 Sustainability technology
* 🚗 Mobility & automotive technology
* 🏙️ Civic technology
* 🌦️ Climate & weather intelligence
* ♻️ Waste management
* 🔬 Applied research
* 💻 Product engineering
* 🚀 Startup & innovation ecosystems

**YZ! is not just about building websites.**

It is about experimenting with how **young technical minds can become part of solving actual industry and societal problems.**
